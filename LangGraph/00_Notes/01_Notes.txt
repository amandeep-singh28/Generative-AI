# LangGraph

## Generative AI

It is a branch of AI that can create new content. It learns patterns from data and uses those patterns to generate something new — such as text, images, audio, code or videos.

For example:
1. LLM-based apps like ChatGPT.
2. Diffusion models for images.
3. Code-generating LLMs like CodeLlama.
4. TTS models like ElevenLabs. (Text to Speech — TTS)
5. Video generation models like Sora.

## Traditional AI vs Generative AI

Traditional AI learns patterns from existing data and uses them to make predictions or decisions.

Historical customer data
↓
ML model
↓
Prediction

Generative AI learns patterns in its training data and uses them to generate new samples.

Traditional AI → often predicts or classifies.
Generative AI → creates new content.
LangGraph → coordinates workflow execution.

# Introduction to Agentic AI

## A normal LLM call looks like this:

User prompt
↓
LLM generate response
↓
Final answer

## An agentic AI workflow can look like this:

User prompt
↓
LLM decides what action is needed
↓
Tool call (if required)
↓
Tool result returned to LLM
↓
Final answer

An agentic system can choose actions towards a goal and adapt what it does based on the situation or results.

Goal → Decide → Act → Evaluate → Decide Again

If the AI can select between different actions based on the situation, that is agentic behaviour.

**Definition:** Agentic AI is an AI that takes a goal, decides what actions to take, and works toward completing that goal with minimal human guidance.

## For example:

→ Generative AI

"Why might a data pipeline fail?"

The AI generates an explanation based on its knowledge.

→ Agentic AI

"Investigate why my data pipeline failed."

The AI might:
1. Examine the logs.
2. Decide what diagnostic to run.
3. Analyze the result.
4. Try another diagnostic (if required).

(Working towards a goal by choosing and adapting actions)

**Imp**

## Note

(i) Is a tool necessary for agentic AI? No

(ii) Can tools make an agent more capable? Yes

(iii) Does using a tool automatically make AI agentic? No

(iv) What is the key idea? The AI can choose and adapt its actions towards a goal.

Agentic AI can work in a cycle, it observes results, decides what to do next, and continues investigating based on what it learns.

## Key Characteristics of Agentic AI

(i) Autonomous

(ii) Goal oriented

(iii) Planning

(iv) Reasoning

(v) Adaptability

(vi) Context awareness

## (i) Autonomy

Agentic AI can work towards a goal without requiring a human to tell it every single step.

- **Without autonomy**

  You tell the AI to inspect the logs, then tell it to check the logs, then tell it to check the databases, then tell it what to do next.

- **With autonomy**

  You gave it the goal, and it chooses the next step within the permissions and rules you have defined.

- **Autonomy can be controlled**

  Autonomy does not mean giving AI unlimited freedom.

  (i) Permission scope

  (ii) Human in the loop (HITL)

  Human approval is needed.

  (iii) Override controls.

  Human can interrupt or change what the AI is doing.

- **Autonomy can be dangerous**

  Imagine a recruitment AI that can act independently. If its rules or decisions are flawed, it might:

  (i) Send job offers with incorrect salary details.

  (ii) Unfairly shortlisting students.

Autonomy means the AI can act independently, but its permissions, decisions, and actions should be controlled appropriately.


## (ii) Goal Oriented

Being goal oriented means that the AI system operates with a persistent objective in mind and continuously directs its actions to achieve that objective, rather than just responding to isolated prompts.

→ Goals act as a compass for autonomy.

→ Goals can come with constraints. {SHMC backed engineer from Andhra only?}

→ Goals are stored in core memory.

→ Goals can be altered.

## (iii) Planning

Planning means breaking a big goal into smaller actions and choosing a suitable way to achieve it.

### Step 1 → Generate Multiple Plans

AI considers different ways to achieve the same goal.

Plan A → Post job on LinkedIn.

Plan B → Use employee referrals.

### Step 2 → Evaluate Each Plan

- Cost?
- Efficiency?
- Risk?
- Constraints?

### Step 3 → Select the Best Plan

AI chooses a plan based on evaluation.

**Two ways**

↓  

**Multiple plans**

AI considers alternatives (Plan A, Plan B).

**Strategic planning**

Execute the plan, examine the result and revise its plan (if necessary).

## (iv) Reasoning

It means analyzing information, connecting facts, and using them to reach a conclusion or make a decision.

**Planning**

↳ Decides what steps to take.

**Reasoning**

↳ Analyzes information to reach conclusions.

## (v) Adaptability

*Changing your approach based on new info*

It means the AI can change its approach when it receives new information or when its current approach doesn't work.

## (vi) Context Awareness

The AI understands the current situation by using relevant information from the task, previous task and available memory.

It is implemented through memory.
